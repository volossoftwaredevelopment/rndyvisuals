    SHOT FROM THE WATER, NOT THE SHORE.

    I'm Ivan — a videographer based in Greece, working on and around the water.

    For the past several seasons I've filmed sailing yachts, motor yachts, regattas and the coastlines between them — from the deck, the tender and the air. My job is simple to say and hard to do: make a vessel feel the way it feels to be aboard. The light at seven in the morning. The sound of the hull at speed. The quiet after the sails are trimmed. I keep crews small, schedules tight and edits honest. If it floats, I'll find the film in it.
