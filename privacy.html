Privacy Policy

Last updated — 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how rndyvisuals ("I", "me", "the site") handles information when you visit this website or get in touch. I keep things minimal: I don't run accounts, I don't sell data, and I only collect what's needed to run the site and answer you.

Information I collect

Information you give me. When you contact me by email, WhatsApp or Instagram, I receive whatever you choose to send — typically your name, contact details and a description of your project. I use it only to reply and to work with you.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, this site may collect basic technical information through cookies and analytics — for example the pages you view, your approximate region, and your device and browser type. This is aggregated and helps me understand how the site is used. It does not identify you personally.

Cookies

The site uses a small number of cookies: one to remember that you've seen the cookie notice, and analytics cookies to measure traffic. You can accept the notice, and you can clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking them won't break the site.

How I use information

  • To respond to your messages and discuss your project.
  • To understand which work resonates and improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

Footage and photography

Films and photographs I create for clients are handled under our separate agreement. I won't publish footage that features you or your event without permission, and you can ask me to take down work that features you at any time.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, embeds or serves video, and may use a privacy-minded analytics provider. These services process limited technical data on my behalf. I do not sell or rent your information to anyone. Links to Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp lead to those platforms, which have their own privacy policies.

Data retention

I keep messages and project files only as long as needed for our work together and my records, and analytics data only in aggregate. You can ask me to delete correspondence that concerns you.

Your rights

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Just email hello@rndyvisuals.com and I'll take care of it.

Children

This site isn't directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
